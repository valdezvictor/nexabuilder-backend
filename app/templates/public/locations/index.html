Frequently Asked Questions

How does NexaBuilder match me with a licensed contractor?

NexaBuilder uses a scoring engine that evaluates CSLB license status, specialty trade, project type, location proximity, and verified reviews to rank the best-matched contractors for your specific project. You receive up to three matched bids within 48 hours.

Are all contractors on NexaBuilder CSLB licensed?

Yes. Every contractor in the NexaBuilder network is verified against the California Contractors State License Board (CSLB) database. We check license status, insurance, and bonding before any contractor can receive project referrals.

Which California counties does NexaBuilder serve?

NexaBuilder currently serves Los Angeles County, Orange County, San Diego County, Riverside County, San Bernardino County, and Ventura County, with over 243,000 CSLB-verified contractors across these regions.

Is there a fee to use NexaBuilder as a homeowner?

No. NexaBuilder is completely free for homeowners and property owners. You submit your project, receive matched contractor bids, and choose with no fees or obligations. Contractors pay a performance-based referral fee only when a project is awarded.

¿NexaBuilder tiene contratistas que hablan español?

Sí. NexaBuilder ofrece servicio bilingüe en inglés y español. Muchos de nuestros contratistas verificados son hispanohablantes y podemos hacer coincidir su proyecto con contratistas que se comunican en español.
